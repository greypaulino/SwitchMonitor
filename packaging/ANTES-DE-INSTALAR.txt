SwitchMonitor 1.10.0 - Windows 10/11, 64-bit

AutoHotkey and ControlMyMonitor are included. Installation is for your Windows user and does not change graphics drivers.

LG 29WK600 with Dell Intel HD 3000: switching to DP has been verified. On the tested AMD PC, switching back to HDMI 1 also works. The Dell resends HDMI 1 after its connection returns to synchronize the monitor menu.

Other monitors depend on their DDC/CI support. Switching to another computer can interrupt control from the previous computer.

Your settings are preserved during updates and uninstall.
